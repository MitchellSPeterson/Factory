Visitor mode: Operate
Audience: the Factory operator at this machine
Job: scan live Jobs, open the one that needs you, or start a new Job
Constraints: keep the card grid; keep search, Workflow, Progress, and Lane; no seven-column board; keep VASA chrome and CONTEXT.md language; do not restyle Job detail or New job

## Direction contract

THESIS: Jobs is a scannable three-up card grid of live work. It refuses a seven-column Lane board and refuses hiding search or filters. Attention is sort order plus a quiet card tint, not a second badge on every card.

OWN-WORLD: VASA dark Notara sidebar, system sans, 1px hairlines, 12px card radii, pill chips, violet on the selected Progress chip and New job. Amber and danger tints only on cards that need the operator. No new identity.

STORY: Open Jobs, read how many need you, scan readable request titles, open that Job, or start a new one. Search, Workflow, Progress, and Lane stay on the page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Unchanged sidebar. Heading Jobs, one-line count, New job top right. Search leading a filter bar; Progress as chips; Workflow and Lane as selects. Three-up cards: Project only in View all, wrapping title, Lane badge, Workflow and age. Attention cards first with a warmer surface. Loading is skeleton cards. Empty teaches New job.

FORM: Polished card grid, locked by the operator. No concept-seed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
